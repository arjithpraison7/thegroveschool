A healthy mind in a healthy body: the school has programmes for the physical fitness of every student, and regular games periods, twice a week, are part of the school timetable.

Students have the opportunity to play:

- Cricket
- Basketball
- Football
- Chess
- Badminton

**Special coaching** in cricket and basketball is held from 2:30 to 4:00 PM every day, Monday to Friday.

**The Annual Sports Day** is conducted every academic year and is always a gala affair. A separate playground gives the younger children their own space for outdoor play.
