Student elections are held every year for various posts, and children learn to exercise their franchise. The academic year starts with the investiture ceremony, in which a new body of prefects takes the oath to lead the student body to new heights of excellence in sports, cultural and academic events.

There are four houses, and inter-house competitions range from sports and culture to academic events.
