We are happy to highlight the achievements of The Grove School from 1 June 2016 to 31 May 2017.

## ISC affiliation

On 10 April 2017, the Council approved and granted ISC affiliation to The Grove School, so that admissions to Class XI could commence for the academic year 2017–18. The school began admissions into Class XI for the new academic year 2017–18.

## Board exam results

The Grove School secured a 100% pass in the ICSE Class X board exam conducted in March 2017, for the sixth consecutive time. All our students secured first class with distinction, which was remarkable. This was achieved through the dedication of the teachers and the hard work and effort of our students.

## School achievements

- This academic year, The Grove School was appointed as the ICSE evaluation centre for Sanskrit and as the examination centre for The Ashram School ICSE.
- The Grove School emerged as the overall champions in the interschool competition conducted by GRT Mahalakshmi Vidyalaya Matriculation Higher Secondary School.
- As part of The C.P. Ramaswami Aiyar Foundation's Golden Jubilee celebrations, The Grove School hosted the Golden Jubilee Interschool Chess Championship for the first time. 29 schools participated.
- In the Kedar Cricket Academy Tournament, played between The Grove School and the Kedar Cricket Academy team, The Grove School won the match.
