Socially Useful Productive Work (SUPW) and care for the environment are part of school life at The Grove. Horticulture is on the syllabus, and children are encouraged to take up gardening and help beautify the campus.

The Eco-Club and the Nature Club involve students in looking after their surroundings, and students visit the Blue Cross of India, where they are taught to love and care for animals.
