**Art.** Drawing and painting are regular subjects in the curriculum. Students' work is displayed in the school's annual magazine, *The Sacred Grove*.

**Indian culture.** Art and architecture, the performing arts, symbolism, festivals and much more are taught through video films, site visits, field trips, lectures and quizzes.

**Music and dance.** Classical music and classical dance are taught twice a week to all students.
