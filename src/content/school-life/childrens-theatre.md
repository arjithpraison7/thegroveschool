A geodesic dome, gifted by the Ministry of Environment and Forests, Government of India, in 1990, has been converted into a children's theatre in connection with the centenary celebration of the late Shri A.R. Jagannathan (1925–2025), former President of The C.P. Ramaswami Aiyar Foundation, whose centenary year was 2025.

Film shows are regularly conducted for students of The Grove School.
